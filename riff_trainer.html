Songs Folder
Built-in songs now live as individual .json files in a folder called songs, next to riff_trainer.html — not baked into the app. Click "Load Songs Folder" (top of the Practice tab) and select that folder; if Brave grants write permission, BPM edits also save to those JSON files. The browser may ask you to renew permission on a future visit. Each file's "type" field ("lead" or "chords") decides how it's practiced — no manual mode toggle needed.
